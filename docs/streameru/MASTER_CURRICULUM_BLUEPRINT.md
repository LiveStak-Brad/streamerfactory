## Overall Educational Philosophy

### 1. Problem-first, not topic-first
Every lesson answers **one clear creator problem** a real streamer faces this week. If it does not change behavior on LIVE, it does not ship.

### 2. Core is sacred
The first 24 lessons are not redesigned, moved, or renamed. Mastery Paths **extend** Core skills; they do not re-teach them. Callbacks only.

### 3. Practice over theory
StreamerU is a doing academy. Study leads to LIVE application. Certificates prove demonstrated skill, not attendance.

### 4. Prefer fewer excellent lessons
Merge overlap. Kill filler. One concept per lesson. Depth over breadth.

### 5. Creator excellence only
We teach creators to become exceptional LIVE creators.  
**We do not teach:** agency ownership, recruiting for competing networks, or how to start/operate creator agencies.

### 6. Progressive professionalization
Beginner → competent operator → professional systems → specialist mastery → sustainable career. Each stage unlocks the next with clear prerequisites.

### 7. Specialty without fragmentation
Music LIVE is a real specialty path. Everything else is craft that any creator can use. Paths are modular; creators specialize after Advanced Creator.

### 8. Safety and sustainability are career skills
Account safety, privacy, copyright, burnout, and pacing are not “soft extras.” They protect the career Core builds.

### 9. Every lesson must produce a tangible outcome
A lesson never ends with “Now you know.”  
It ends with “Now you have built something that makes you a better creator.”

When someone finishes a lesson, they leave with **at least one** of:

- A checklist  
- A worksheet  
- A template  
- A completed action  
- A system  
- A scorecard  
- A downloadable resource  
- A LIVE mission  
- A quiz  
- A portfolio artifact  
- A measurable improvement  

If a draft lesson has no tangible output, it is not ready to ship.

### 10. Advanced Creator is the black belt
Core teaches **how to stream.**  
Advanced Creator teaches **how professionals operate.**  
Mastery Paths are **specializations** after that psychological transition — not random electives bolted onto beginner skills.

### 11. Growth ≠ Content Creation
**Growth** is getting discovered.  
**Content Creation** is becoming worth watching.  
Those are different skills and remain separate Mastery Paths.

### 12. Presence before Growth
New creators fail because they freeze, don’t know what to say, aren’t entertaining, or aren’t memorable — not because they lack analytics.  
Ship and recommend: **Presence → Content Creation → Growth.**

### 13. Capstones build real artifacts; Labs are optional honors
Every Mastery Path ends by **building something real** (Capstone Project) as part of earning the certificate.  
**Labs** are optional honors after the certificate — college-lab / internship energy, never a diploma gate.

### 14. Architecture is locked
From v2.0 forward, every new lesson, quiz, worksheet, mission, Lab, and certification **fills this blueprint**. It does not redesign it.
